— about

Grown from the ground up

Verdant & Stem began in a 300-square-foot shed behind a neighborhood market. We still pick, condition, and arrange everything in-house.

Walk-ins are welcome. If you’re ordering for an event, give us three days and a short description of the space — we’ll put together something specific rather than generic.
